# How We Use AI

The **EC2U PhD Agreements Tool** uses artificial intelligence (AI) to analyse legal documents — specifically, PhD
agreements — in relation to local policies and regulations. Our AI tools help to:

- **Interpret and summarise legal agreements** - AI reviews PhD agreements to extract key terms, obligations, and rights
- **Check compliance** - AI compares agreement terms against relevant local policies to identify potential
  inconsistencies or compliance issues
- **Highlight important clauses** - AI flags sections that may require special attention or further review
- **Support multilingual analysis** - AI assists in processing documents in multiple languages, helping to ensure
  consistent understanding across institutions

# Using AI Analysis Responsibly

## Do Not Rely Solely on AI-Generated Analysis for Legal or Academic Decisions

AI-generated summaries and compliance checks are intended as helpful guides, but may contain errors, omissions, or
outdated information. Always consult the original documents and, when necessary, seek advice from qualified legal or
academic professionals.

## Your Responsibilities

By using this website, you agree to:

- Treat AI-generated analysis as a starting point, not a substitute for professional review
- Verify all important findings with the original legal documents and official sources
- Consult legal or academic experts for critical decisions
- Report any errors, inaccuracies, or issues you encounter

## Our Liability

We are not responsible for errors, omissions, or inaccuracies in AI-generated analyses. You accept the risks of relying
on this information and are responsible for verifying it before making decisions.

## Content Changes

AI-generated analyses may be updated or removed at any time without notice.
